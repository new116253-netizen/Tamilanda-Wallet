Privacy Principles

Stored on this device
Wallet records are stored locally in browser storage rather than being automatically sent to a remote finance service by this page.
☁️
Backup is under your control
Create your own JSON backup when needed. Keep that file somewhere safe because it can contain your wallet records.
🗑️
Deleted records use Trash
Records moved to Trash remain recoverable until you permanently delete them or empty the Trash.
